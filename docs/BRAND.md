# Valcerenne brand identity

## Naming shortlist

Initial exact-name web checks were performed on 23 October 2026. These are discovery checks, not registry clearance or a domain registration check. No name or domain has been purchased.

| Candidate | Character and pronunciation | Wordmark / international use / expansion | Initial conflict consideration | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VALCERENNE | val-seh-REN; composed, distinctive, architectural | A balanced sequence of Roman capitals; no accents or category restriction; suitable across wardrobe, home and technology | No obvious exact-name result surfaced in the initial search; domain availability and formal clearance unverified | Selected working brand |
| VEYRANEL | vay-rah-NEL; individual, slightly more enigmatic | Strong V opening, memorable rhythm, flexible across categories; pronunciation needs more explanation | Search surfaced historical place-name usage, not an obvious exact luxury retailer | Runner-up; less immediate pronunciation |

| AVENOIRE | av-eh-NWAR; evocative evening mood | Memorable but French pronunciation and narrow dark-fashion association | Existing retailer https://avenoire.fr/pages/qui-sommes-nous and Maison Avenoire | Rejected |

Elvarenne was a provisional direction before the search revealed the conflict; it was replaced before artwork generation. The final site consistently uses Valcerenne. A specialist should clear the chosen name in relevant territories and product/retail classes before a public commercial launch. Similar spelling and phonetic conflicts remain unassessed.

## Identity

Official working name: VALCERENNE. Tagline: **The art of choosing well.**

Positioning: a considered multi-category retailer for people who prefer enduring design and a distinctive point of view. The existing catalogue spans women's and men's fashion, footwear, accessories, home, technology, and fitness. The brand is a retailer, not a claim that it manufactures the supplied products.

Audience: design-aware adults building a wardrobe and home with intention. India and INR remain the current commercial context; the identity can travel internationally without falsely promising international fulfilment.

Personality: composed, observant, warm, precise. Confident without exclusivity claims, invented heritage, social proof, manufactured scarcity or affiliation with other luxury houses.

Brand story: A beautiful life is built one considered choice at a time. Valcerenne brings the things we wear, use, and live with into one point of view. We look for the detail that makes a piece worth a second look: a silhouette, a texture, a useful idea. The collection invites people to choose deliberately, discover their own taste, and find beauty in everyday rituals. It is a new perspective, not a fabricated centuries-old maison.

Primary type: Cormorant Garamond, regular and italic for editorial headings. Secondary type: DM Sans, 400–600 for navigation, labels, forms and shopping information. Georgia and system sans-serif provide fallbacks. Google Fonts remain external assets, using display=swap.

The wordmark uses a widely spaced Roman serif and an original folded V resting on a small architectural baseline. The generated raster is the primary header asset. Companion SVG artwork provides crisp scalable light, dark, monochrome and icon forms. The SVG is a deliberate digital companion, not a claim of automatic perfect vectorization of the generated bitmap. SVG wordmarks use a Georgia-family text fallback; convert to outlined artwork with a licensed font before professional print production.

Use ivory/oxblood on light backgrounds and ivory on deep olive or ink. Keep clear space at least the monogram's height around primary artwork. Header logo width is 236px on desktop and 174px on mobile. Use the monogram alone below wordmark legibility size. Never stretch, bevel, shadow, apply gradients, or enclose the wordmark in a generic badge.

Photography: warm limestone, natural materials, directional daylight, earth tones, quiet tailoring, intentional crops. The locally generated hero is campaign imagery, not a new product listing. Editorial collection imagery comes from Unsplash. Existing catalogue photography is preserved, including legacy inconsistencies; verify product-image accuracy and commercial usage permissions before launch.

Icons: fine, simple Font Awesome forms from the existing dependency. Avoid emoji in product-discovery controls. Motion: brief image zooms and colour changes, no countdowns or scroll spectacle. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Layout: generous margins, thin rules, square controls, uncluttered product cards, and responsive two-column mobile grids.

Voice: concise and concrete. Explain material and function only when supported by product records. Never invent certification, testimonials, provenance, delivery promises, inventory, returns guarantees or payment confirmation.
