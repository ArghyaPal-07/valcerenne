The details, considered.

Delivery & returns

Prices are in Indian rupees. Standard shipping is ₹149, complimentary for orders of ₹999 or more. Shipping and estimated tax are shown before you submit your order. Contact client services to confirm delivery availability, timing, or a return before purchasing. No automatic return window or international shipping service is currently configured.

Payment information

Online card and wallet payments are not connected. Never enter card details on this site. Checkout supports cash on delivery, recorded as payment pending; availability must be confirmed with client services. An order confirmation records your order, not a completed payment.

Privacy

Firebase Authentication manages sign-in. Profiles, addresses, wishlists, orders and newsletter preferences are stored in Cloud Firestore. Firebase persists your sign-in in this browser; local storage keeps your shopping session; session storage keeps your checkout summary. These are used for the shopping experience. Contact client services about access, correction, newsletter removal, or deletion requests. No analytics service has been added.

Shopping terms

The existing catalogue supplies product descriptions, prices, variants, and availability. Campaign photographs are editorial imagery and do not represent additional inventory. Review your selection and shipping details before submitting an order. Product availability is checked by the server. Delivery and returns require operational confirmation from the store.
